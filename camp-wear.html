Day by day

The fits, mapped out

Three days, five fits. Here's the quick rundown — the full LookBook below has the details and inspiration.

Friday · Day One

Street wear, then chill

First night is a chill night — stretch your legs, get settled, take it easy.

Daytime

  • Nice pair of sneakers
  • Fire pair of pants
  • Tuff t-shirt

Nighttime

  • Sweatpants or shorts
  • Running shoes
  • Breathable shirt
Saturday · Day Two

Jersey day → Cowboy Night

Saturday night is a complete flip of the switch — it's Cowboy Night, and your fit matters.

Daytime — Camp Jersey Day

  • Camp jersey
  • Fire pair of pants
  • Nice pair of shoes
  • Extra athletic wear — you'll need it!

Nighttime — Cowboy / Denim Night

  • Jeans!
  • Denim!
  • Denim & jeans!
Sunday · Day Three

Sunday Best

Come dressed to impress. Our time together ends, but the drip doesn't.

Morning

  • The nicest clothes you have
  • Whatever style suits you best
  • Be ready to outdress or be outdressed — in the most church way possible
Remember: this is only a guide — feel free to style yourself how you see fit. Just remember, modesty is always the best policy. We can't wait to see who wears it best at camp. — The Trail Guides
